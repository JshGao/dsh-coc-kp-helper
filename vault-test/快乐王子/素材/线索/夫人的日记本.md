## 提示词

An opened leather-bound diary, transparent background, isolated object, top-down view: aged yellowed paper, dense illegible handwriting in dark ink, a page scored through by a sharp object, a pale yellow-brown stain near the torn lower corner, a small brass clasp. The cover is dark green with worn gilding. Raking warm lamp light from the upper left. Painterly digital illustration, high detail, transparent background, no background scenery, no readable text.

## 中文翻译

一本摊开的皮面日记，透明背景，孤立物件，俯视：纸张发黄，密集而无法辨认的墨迹，其中一页被锐物刮破，破损的下角附近有一块淡黄褐色的污渍，小铜扣。封面深绿，烫金已磨损。暖色台灯从左上方斜照。绘画感数字插画，高细节，透明背景，没有背景景物，不出现可读文字。

## 要点

- 必须出现：被锐物刮破的纸面、暗黄污渍、铜扣
- 不能出现：可辨认的长段文字、背景桌面
